# Emphatic Labs — brand notes

## Name

**Emphatic Labs**

"Labs" is the best fit for what this actually is: one company, several products in development
at once. It makes that sound deliberate rather than unfocused, and it scales — Emphatic Labs is
the parent, each startup keeps its own product name underneath it.

Rejected: *Solutions* (reads as consulting/IT services, implies billing clients hourly),
*Tech* (generic), *Software* (flat for a parent company), *Studio* (reads as client work).

## Positioning

**Tagline:** Advancing the integration of AI into consumer software.

**Supporting line:** Emphatic Labs is a software company building its own products.

That's the whole message. Nothing about vision, mission, or how we work — there's no product
to point at yet, so the site says what the company is aimed at and stops. Copy stays short and
factual everywhere; length is what makes a pre-launch site read as filler.

## Logo and artwork

The mark is a green bottle mid-splash. It carries two ideas at once — something being
poured out, and something with fizz — which suits a company that hasn't launched yet.
It sits in the navbar at 34px next to the wordmark.

The matching pour artwork runs along the bottom of the home hero. It was drawn with empty
space at the left, so headline and buttons sit in the clear area and never collide with it.
Home page only — the inner pages stay plain so the artwork keeps its impact.

The navbar carries a field of small green pixels drifting slowly rightward. Low opacity,
2–4px squares, drawn on whole-pixel boundaries so they stay crisp. It freezes for
`prefers-reduced-motion` and stops when the tab is hidden.

## Visual direction

Simplicity modeled on deepseek.com: near-white ground, a soft color wash behind the hero, a
small wordmark, one large light-weight headline, and a couple of small rounded cards. Generous
whitespace, hairline borders, no decoration.

**Type:** Outfit Light (300) for headlines — large and airy, never bold. Manrope for body,
navigation, and UI.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#ffffff` | Page |
| Surface | `#f4faf6` | Hover states, tinted panels |
| Line | `#e3ede7` | Borders, dividers |
| Line strong | `#d2e3d8` | Inputs, secondary buttons |
| Text | `#0f1512` | Headings and body (near-black, slight green bias) |
| Muted | `#5f6f66` | Secondary text |
| **Accent** | **`#0e7a45`** | Links, solid buttons, the accent panel — passes AA on white |
| Accent bright | `#2ec27e` | Logo mark, hero wash, focus rings |
| Accent soft | `#eaf7f0` | Tag pills |

Two greens on purpose: the bright one is decorative only, the deep one is anything carrying
text. Using the bright green for text would fail contrast.

## Voice

Short, plain, declarative. State what's true now rather than manufacturing traction. Avoid
"innovative", "cutting-edge", "solutions", "revolutionizing".

## Domain

Site assumes `emphaticlabs.com` for its email addresses. **Not yet verified as available** —
check before anything gets printed. Fallbacks: `emphatic.dev`, `emphaticlabs.io`, `emphatic.co`.
